Article 2

Purpose and description of the Service

MakerStock is a SaaS (Software as a Service) production management application designed for BigCommerce merchants. It enables, in particular:

  • Management of raw material inventory, including batch and expiry date traceability (depending on the subscribed Plan)
  • Creation and management of manufacturing recipes (Bill of Materials — BOM)
  • Planning and execution of production runs
  • Automatic cost of goods sold (COGS) calculation
  • Two-way synchronisation with the BigCommerce catalogue and sales
  • Supplier procurement management (depending on the subscribed Plan)
