2.3 Data received via BigCommerce

As part of the native integration with BigCommerce, MakerStock receives, via the BigCommerce API and with the user's consent at the time of installation, the following data:

  • Product catalogue (name, SKU, retail price, BigCommerce internal identifiers)
  • Store information (store name, currency, BC identifier)
  • Order data (quantities sold per product, necessary for automatic stock deduction)
ℹ️
We do not receive the personal data of your BigCommerce store's end customers (buyer names, addresses or emails). Only the data necessary for the production management Service to function is transmitted.

2.4 Data we do not collect

MakerStock does not and will never collect:

  • Bank card details or payment credentials (managed exclusively by BigCommerce)
  • Health data or special categories of data within the meaning of Article 9 GDPR
  • Biometric data
  • Data relating to persons under 16 years of age

Article 8

Specific rights — California residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident (United States), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), grants you additional rights over your personal data:

  • Right to know: know the categories and purposes of data collected, the sources and any recipients.
  • Right to delete: request deletion of your data, subject to applicable legal exceptions.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing: MakerStock does not sell or share your personal data for advertising purposes. This right is therefore not applicable in the current context of the Service.
  • Right to non-discrimination: you will not be discriminated against for having exercised your CCPA/CPRA rights.
  • Right to correction: request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive data: MakerStock does not collect sensitive personal data within the meaning of the CPRA.

To exercise these rights, send your request to contact@makerstocks.com, stating your California residency. We will respond within the 45-day period provided by the CCPA, extendable by a further 45 days if necessary with prior notification.

Article 10

Security

MakerStock implements appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss, unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction, in accordance with Article 32 GDPR. These measures include in particular:

  • Encryption of data in transit via TLS 1.2 minimum
  • Encryption of sensitive data at rest
  • Authentication delegated to BigCommerce via OAuth 2.0 — MakerStock does not store your passwords
  • Access to production data restricted to authorised personnel on a least-privilege basis
  • Regular backups on EU-hosted infrastructure
  • Logging of access and sensitive actions

In the event of a data breach likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms, we undertake to notify you as soon as possible in accordance with Article 34 GDPR, and to notify the relevant supervisory authority within the 72-hour period required by Article 33.

No security measure is infallible. MakerStock cannot guarantee the absolute security of data transmitted over the internet. We recommend that you never share your login credentials and immediately report any suspicious access to contact@makerstocks.com.

Article 11

Minors

The MakerStock Service is intended for professionals and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from persons under 16 years of age. If you are a minor, please do not use the Service and do not provide us with any personal data.

If we learn that a minor under 16 has provided us with personal data without verifiable parental consent, we will take the necessary steps to delete that data as soon as possible. If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe your child has sent us data, please contact us at contact@makerstocks.com.
