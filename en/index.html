    How it works

    From raw material to the sold product

    MakerStock automates the link between your supplier, your workshop and your BigCommerce store.

    1
    Goods reception
    Supplier deliveries are entered with lot numbers and expiry dates. Stock is credited immediately.
    2
    Planned production
    Schedule runs from the desk. Your operators execute them from the workshop via mobile.
    3
    Stock auto-credited
    At run completion, materials are deducted FIFO. Finished goods stock is credited.
    4
    Sale = deduction
    Every BigCommerce sale deducts the corresponding materials. Zero double entry.
    Production

    Your recipes.
    Your pace.

    Create your BOMs in minutes. MakerStock computes the cost of goods sold (COGS) for every recipe on the fly, batch by batch. Schedule runs from the desk, let your operators execute them from the workshop via the dedicated mobile interface.

    • Unlimited recipes with COGS computed on the fly
    • Planned or immediate runs — smart queue
    • Dedicated mobile interface for field operators
    • CSV traceability export on every completed run
    Traceability

    Each lot.
    Each gram.

    Every entry creates a lot with supplier number, expiry date and snapshot cost. Consumption follows FIFO automatically and prioritizes lots closest to their expiry date to reduce losses. Every gram stays traceable from the lot to the run to the finished product, with COGS recalculated at every step.

    • Traceability lot → run → finished product → customer
    • Low stock and forecast alerts (30s refresh)
    • Targeted lot write-off — loss, breakage, quality control
    • Allergens + INCI sheet per raw material
    Interface mobile

    The workshop in
    your pocket.

    Your operators don't need a computer. The mobile shell covers exactly what they need in the field, without access to financial data or configuration.

    • Start / complete a run in 3 taps
    • Receive a delivery with batch & expiry date entry
    • View and prepare orders
